Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PocketRule ("PocketRule", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the PocketRule mobile application.

Privacy by default. PocketRule is designed as a local-first budgeting and planning app. PocketRule does not require a user account and does not require access to your bank account.

1. Information We Collect

PocketRule is designed to minimize the information it collects. The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, home address, or other identity information to use its core budgeting features.

Depending on how you use the app, information may be entered or generated locally, including:

  • Budget and plan amounts.
  • Budget categories and rules you create or select.
  • Spending amounts that you record.
  • Plan dates and other budgeting preferences.
  • Currency preferences and currency information suggested from your device settings.
  • Reminder or notification settings you choose within the app.

This information is intended to remain on your device and is not sent by PocketRule to a PocketRule server for account or advertising purposes.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

PocketRule stores the information needed to provide its budgeting, planning, rule, and spending-tracking features on your device.

Because this information is stored locally, PocketRule does not provide cloud synchronization of your budgets or spending records as part of the app's current core functionality.

If you uninstall the app or clear its application data, locally stored PocketRule information may be removed by the operating system. Device-level backups or operating-system features may be governed by the policies of the platform provider.

3. Notifications and Reminders

PocketRule may allow you to schedule reminders or time-sensitive notifications. These features require Android notification and/or alarm permissions where applicable.

PocketRule uses these permissions only to provide the reminder and notification functionality you request. PocketRule does not use notification permissions to read your private notifications or messages.

You can control notification and alarm permissions through your device settings. If you disable them, scheduled reminders or time-sensitive notifications may not work.

4. Device and Technical Information

PocketRule may use basic device or operating-system settings needed to provide functionality, such as determining a suitable currency or formatting information according to your device's locale settings.

PocketRule does not intentionally collect precise location information, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, camera data, SMS messages, call logs, or bank credentials for its core budgeting features.

5. Bank and Financial Account Access

PocketRule does not connect to your bank account and does not request your bank username, password, card number, or banking credentials.

Any spending or budget figures in PocketRule are information that you choose to enter or manage within the app.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

PocketRule uses Google AdMob to display advertising in eligible areas of the app. Ads are not intentionally shown during onboarding or critical money-entry and budgeting flows.

Because PocketRule uses Google Mobile Ads technology, Google may automatically process certain information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Depending on the applicable Google SDK configuration and your choices, this can include information such as your device IP address, interactions with the app, diagnostic or performance information, and device or advertising identifiers.

Advertising and consent choices can affect whether ads are personalized. Where required, PocketRule uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow. You can also manage relevant advertising and privacy controls through Android and Google settings.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Analytics and Tracking

PocketRule does not intentionally use third-party advertising trackers or cross-app tracking technologies to profile users.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, cloud services, or another third-party service that materially changes how information is handled, we will update this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures before or when those changes become effective.

8. Third-Party Services and Software

PocketRule is built using third-party software libraries and the Android platform. These components help us build, package, and operate the app. PocketRule does not intentionally provide your budgeting information to third parties for advertising or sale.

Third-party platform providers, including Google Play and Android, may process information under their own privacy policies when you download, install, update, or use an Android application. Their practices are outside PocketRule's control.

9. Data Sharing

PocketRule does not sell your personal information.

PocketRule does not share your locally stored budgeting information with advertisers or data brokers as part of the app's current functionality.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, the information you include in your message may be received by us so that we can respond to your request. We may retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain support records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by PocketRule. The app is designed to keep budgeting information on your device rather than transmitting it to a PocketRule cloud account.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are also responsible for protecting access to your device, including using the device's available screen lock and security features.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

PocketRule does not maintain a PocketRule user account containing your budgeting records. Information used by the app's core budgeting features is stored locally on your device for as long as it remains available to the app.

You can remove locally stored app information by using the applicable delete, reset, clear-data, or uninstall controls provided by your Android device, subject to any operating-system backup or restore behavior.

If you contact PocketRule by email, you may request deletion of your support correspondence where legally and operationally feasible.

12. Children's Privacy

PocketRule is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account-based service because PocketRule does not require a user account for its core functionality.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, please contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when PocketRule's features, data practices, services, or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected in the policy before or when the changed practices take effect, where appropriate.
